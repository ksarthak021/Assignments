The house

Since 1990, Shree Vishal Tent Palace has been curating the experience — more than 35 years of making the memories.

The work is physical and sequential: the morning a haldi needs, the height of a mandap, the hour the reception changes temperature, the seat from which an elder can hear the pheras.

How the house works

Signature services

What the house actually builds.

01

Tent and mandap

Canvas engineered for wind, rain, and a rite that cannot be moved. The mandap is drawn with proportion, whether it stands on a lawn, in a courtyard, or inside the tent.

02

Floral and decor

Marigold, mogra, and foliage used as architecture. Garlands, a threshold, a mandap that reads from the last row, and tables that do not compete with the ceremony.

03

Lighting

Daylight for the pheras, a warmer hour at dusk, and a reception that can be seen and heard. Paths stay lit. The mandap is never a dark centre.

04

Staging

The sangeet stage, the haldi, and the reception. Levels, sightlines, and a place for elders to sit without being hidden by a speaker.

05

Guest hospitality

Welcome, water, wayfinding, and a seat that matches the invitation. Travelling guests should know where they are before they are asked to celebrate.

06

Full event direction

The order of the day, rehearsed. Who enters, when the music drops, how the pheras stay audible, and who is holding the next hour.

Planning

Four movements, then the day.

  1. 01

    Listen

    The rites you will keep, the ones you will shorten, and the people who must be close to them. A plan that ignores the family will be decorated and still wrong.

  2. 02

    Walk the ground

    Wind, kitchen access, the place a haldi needs, where elders sit, where rain would travel. The ground decides what the tent can be.

  3. 03

    Draw the room

    Mandap, paths, stage, and light. Flowers follow the drawing. They are not asked to hide a structure that was never resolved.

  4. 04

    Host the day

    A producer holds the order. Guests are received. The next ceremony begins because someone was already there.

The house
