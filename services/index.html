Services

Six crafts, one direction.

Tent and mandap, flowers, light, staging, hospitality, and the person who keeps the day in order. The packages below say how those crafts combine.

01

Tent and mandap

Canvas engineered for wind, rain, and a rite that cannot be moved. The mandap is drawn with proportion, whether it stands on a lawn, in a courtyard, or inside the tent.

02

Floral and décor

Marigold, mogra, and foliage used as architecture. Garlands, a threshold, a mandap that reads from the last row, and tables that do not compete with the ceremony.

03

Lighting

Daylight for the pheras, a warmer hour at dusk, and a reception that can be seen and heard. Paths stay lit. The mandap is never a dark centre.

04

Staging

The sangeet stage, the reception, the baraat’s entrance. Levels, sightlines, and a place for elders to sit without being hidden by a speaker.

05

Guest hospitality

Welcome, water, wayfinding, and a seat that matches the invitation. Travelling guests should know where they are before they are asked to celebrate.

06

Full event direction

The order of the day, rehearsed. Who enters, when the music drops, how the pheras stay audible, and who is holding the next hour.

Questions

Before the first conversation.

How early should a family begin?

For a winter wedding in Jaipur, begin while the date can still move. Grounds and tent crews are promised months ahead. A single ceremony can start closer to the date, once the ground is known.

Do you work only in Jaipur?

The house is in Jaipur. Most gatherings are in the city, on palace grounds, on lawns, and in family courtyards. Work farther out is discussed once the ground and the date are real.

Can the mandap stand in a palace ground, or only under a tent?

Both. The mandap is its own structure. It can stand in a courtyard, on a lawn, or inside a tent dressed to feel like a room. The ground decides the engineering. The rite decides the form.

What separates the three packages?

Nivaas is one ceremony. Darbar is a full wedding day or a large celebration, from procession to reception. Rajmahal is a multi-day weekend with hospitality and one director across sangeet, wedding, and reception. Fees are quoted after a conversation about the ground, the rites, and the season.

How are monsoon and summer dates handled?

Winter is the crowded season. Summer needs shade, water, and a later evening. Monsoon needs a tent that can take rain, covered guest paths, and a ceremony plan if the sky opens. The season is part of the design, which is why the planner asks for it.
