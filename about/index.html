About us

More than 35 years, and the memory of the day.

We have been curating the experience since 1990. A wedding here is sangeet, haldi, pheras, and reception, held as one sequence rather than a single decorated hour. The tent, the mandap, and the decor are how that sequence becomes a room.

The same house keeps celebrations that are not weddings: a birthday, a family day, a hosted evening. We make the memories by keeping the hour, the path, and the light in order, so the people in the room can stay with the rite.

Shree Vishal Tent Palace, also called SVTP Events, is in Jaipur. The house is at Shiv Colony, Kissan Marg, Tonk Phatak, Jaipur 302015.

How the work goes

Listen, walk, draw, host.

  1. 01

    Listen

    The rites you will keep, the ones you will shorten, and the people who must be close to them. A plan that ignores the family will be decorated and still wrong.

  2. 02

    Walk the ground

    Wind, kitchen access, the place a haldi needs, where elders sit, where rain would travel. The ground decides what the tent can be.

  3. 03

    Draw the room

    Mandap, paths, stage, and light. Flowers follow the drawing. They are not asked to hide a structure that was never resolved.

  4. 04

    Host the day

    A producer holds the order. Guests are received. The next ceremony begins because someone was already there.
