Celebrations

Gatherings with the scale of a sangeet.

Birthdays, family days, corporate dinners, and parties large enough to need a stage. The same craft as a wedding, held to the length of a single night or a single afternoon.

Plan a gathering

Social and hosted

A celebration still needs a room.

Without the sequence of a wedding, it is easy to underbuild. Guests still arrive, still need shade and water, still look for the person being honoured. SVTP treats these days as hosted rooms: tent or courtyard, flowers, light, and someone watching the order.

01

Family and social days

An engagement, a naming, a reception that is not attached to a full wedding week. One room, dressed fully, with hospitality that does not feel borrowed from a banquet hall.

02

Birthdays

A milestone that deserves more than a cake table. We scale the tent, the flowers, and the evening so the person being honoured is visible, and the guests are looked after.

03

Corporate gatherings

Dinners, launches, and meetings that host clients in Jaipur. The same discipline as a wedding: a clear entrance, light that can handle a speech, and a run of show.

04

Sangeet-scale parties

Music, dance, and a guest list large enough to need a stage. These nights borrow the energy of a sangeet and the structure of a directed event.

Scale

From a dinner to a night that fills a tent.

Smaller gatherings begin with Nivaas: one dressed room and a producer. A sangeet-scale party, or a guest list that fills the lawn, moves to Darbar so the entrance, the stage, and the hospitality are designed together.
